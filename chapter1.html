Chapter 1: Physical Quantities and Measurement

Easy Physics Notes | GM NOTES

1. Physical Quantity

A physical quantity is a quantity that can be measured and expressed by a number and a unit.

Examples:

  • Length = 5 metres
  • Mass = 10 kilograms
  • Time = 20 seconds
  • Temperature = 30 kelvin

2. Fundamental Quantities

Fundamental quantities are independent quantities. They are not defined in terms of other physical quantities.

3. Derived Quantities

Derived quantities are obtained by combining two or more fundamental quantities.

  • Area = Length × Breadth
  • Volume = Length × Breadth × Height
  • Speed = Distance / Time
  • Density = Mass / Volume
  • Force = Mass × Acceleration

4. International System of Units (SI)

SI stands for International System of Units. It is a standard system of measurement used all over the world.

Examples: metre (m), kilogram (kg), second (s), and ampere (A).

5. Measuring Instruments

  • Metre Rule: Used to measure length.
  • Measuring Tape: Used to measure longer distances.
  • Stopwatch: Used to measure time.
  • Beam Balance: Used to measure mass.
  • Vernier Calipers: Used to measure small lengths and diameters.
  • Screw Gauge: Used to measure very small thicknesses and diameters.

6. Scientific Notation

Scientific notation is a short way of writing very large or very small numbers.

It is written in the form:

N × 10ⁿ

Example:

  • 300000 = 3 × 10⁵
  • 0.0005 = 5 × 10⁻⁴

9. Important MCQs

1. What is the SI unit of length?

A) Kilogram   B) Metre   C) Second   D) Ampere

Answer: B) Metre

2. What is the SI unit of mass?

A) Metre   B) Second   C) Kilogram   D) Kelvin

Answer: C) Kilogram

3. Which instrument measures time?

A) Stopwatch   B) Beam Balance   C) Metre Rule   D) Screw Gauge

Answer: A) Stopwatch

4. What is the SI unit of temperature?

A) Celsius   B) Kelvin   C) Joule   D) Watt

Answer: B) Kelvin

5. Which quantity is derived?

A) Length   B) Mass   C) Time   D) Speed

Answer: D) Speed
